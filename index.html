How to play Ultimate

  1. Your move picks their board

    Where you play inside a small board decides which small board your opponent must play in next. Play the top-right square, and they play in the top-right board. X starts, and the first move can go anywhere.

  2. Win small boards

    Get three in a row inside a small board to claim it with a big X or O. A claimed board is closed, and no more moves can be made in it. A full board with no line is a tie and belongs to nobody.

  3. Free turn

    If you are sent to a board that is already won, full or tied, you get a free turn. Play in any open square on any open board.

  4. Win the game

    Claim three small boards in a row on the big grid. If the whole grid fills up with no line, whoever claimed the most small boards wins. Equal boards is a draw.
